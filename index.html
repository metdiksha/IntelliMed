IntelliMed

A platform developed for Thapar Health Centre to make it easier for patients to manage their health records, book appointments, and access vital information while enabling the dispensary staff to efficiently manage their operations. This also aims to integrate AI/ML in the heathcare industry.

What we do?

The Health Centre, TIET serves as a critical provider of basic medical services for both staff and students, functioning as a vital resource within the Institute premises.
Equipped with restroom and dressing room facilities, as well as a fully operational dispensary, it is staffed by a dedicated team of medical professionals who prioritize the well-being and safety of all stakeholders.

  • Number of people reached

    20,000

  • Number of people served

    10,000

  • Number of medical team members

    17

  • Enhance Overall Productivity

    Ensure good health for students, teaching staff, and non-teaching staff through basic medical care and counseling.

  • Raise Health Awareness

    Educate and inform the Thapar community about current and relevant health issues.

  • Basic Health Care Services:

    Provide immediate care for minor illnesses and injuries.

  • Prompt and Early Referral:

    Refer patients to specialized care when necessary for more serious health concerns.
